runner/execution: launch models
===============================

Once :doc:`data_preparation` is complete, follow :doc:`runners` for model-specific
settings, working directories, forcing paths, time/restart conventions, dry-run
commands and launch examples. Supply a compiled executable compatible with the
chosen namelist template. The model appendices explain the science and references.
Return to :doc:`runner_workflows` to review the handoff between the two stages.
